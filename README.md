## 📌 Projects

### 📊 [Cardiovascular Disease Prevalence & Stress Test Analysis](./cardiovascular-risk-analysis)
* **Objective:** Clean and analyze clinical data from the UCI Heart Disease dataset to identify key markers of cardiovascular risk.
* **Tools:** Python (`pandas`, `numpy`), Tableau Public.
* **Key Findings:** Patients with heart disease exhibited significantly higher ST depression (`oldpeak`) and lower maximum heart rates (`thalach`) during exercise stress tests.
